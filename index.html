MajuTech Industrial & Engineering

MajuTech Industrial & Engineering specializes in providing high-quality construction, electrical, and mechanical services to both government and private sectors. Our team is committed to delivering efficient, safe, and sustainable engineering solutions that meet client expectations and industry standards.

With a focus on precision, innovation, and teamwork, MajuTech continues to expand its capabilities in infrastructure development, equipment installation, and project management, ensuring every project is completed on time and with excellence.

Teguh Maju Agro Farm

Teguh Maju Agro Farm provides comprehensive plantation management services to FELDA and other agricultural organizations. Our expertise covers land clearing, mature palm tree felling, replanting, maintenance, and crop health management.

We emphasize work quality, timeliness, and adherence to safety and sustainable farming practices. With experienced manpower and modern machinery, Teguh Maju Agro Farm is dedicated to improving palm oil yield, reducing long-term operational costs, and ensuring plantation productivity and sustainability.

Integrated Civil, Mechanical and Electrical Works

Delivering end-to-end engineering solutions that combine civil, mechanical and electrical expertise to meet the demands of Malaysia’s fast-growing infrastructure and industrial sectors.

  • Design and construction of civil structures including drainage, foundation and building works.
  • Installation and maintenance of electrical systems, industrial wiring and street lighting.
  • Mechanical fabrication, piping systems, pump installations and equipment commissioning for industrial and public facilities.

Infrastructure Development & Construction Solutions

We are committed to supporting Malaysia’s development vision through sustainable and innovative infrastructure projects that enhance communities and connectivity.

  • Road and highway construction, earthworks and drainage systems.
  • Public facilities and government building projects compliant with CIDB and MOF standards.
  • Infrastructure rehabilitation, renovation and structural strengthening for existing developments.
  • Use of modern technology and project management systems for efficient, on-time project delivery.

Comprehensive Plantation Management and Development

  • Land clearing, replanting and crop maintenance for oil palm and other commercial plantations.
  • Machinery-assisted felling and waste management following sustainable agricultural practices.
  • Fertilization, pest control and replanting supervision to ensure optimum productivity and yield.

With trained manpower and modern agricultural equipment, we help clients improve plantation efficiency, sustainability and long-term profitability.

Professional and Skilled Manpower Supply

Providing qualified and experienced manpower to meet the growing demand for skilled labor in Malaysia’s construction and engineering sectors.

  • Certified tradesmen including welders, electricians, technicians and machine operators.
  • Temporary and long-term workforce solutions for construction, maintenance and industrial projects.
  • Continuous training and skill development programs to meet CIDB standards and safety compliance.
