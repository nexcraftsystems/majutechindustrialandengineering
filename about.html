Our Story

MajuTech Industrial & Engineering is a forward-thinking organization established in 2020, driven by a team of highly experienced professionals specializing in civil construction, industrial engineering, mechanical and electrical works, and infrastructure development.

MajuTech Industrial & Engineering is a 100% Bumiputera-owned company with recognized Bumiputera Status, registered with the Construction Industry Development Board (CIDB) Malaysia – Grade G3, and the Ministry of Finance (MOF). These accreditations enable us to participate actively and competitively in both government and private sector projects.

Backed by strong financial support, strategic partnerships, and modern, well-maintained equipment, we are fully committed to executing every project with precision, efficiency, and the highest safety and quality standards.
